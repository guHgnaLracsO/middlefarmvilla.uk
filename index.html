Welcome To Middle Farm Villa

A Peaceful Village Retreat in Northamptonshire

A small, friendly family-run bed and breakfast hosted by Jane and John, offering warm hospitality whether traveling for business or pleasure.

Middle Farm Villa B&B is an 18th-century barn conversion located on the village green in Chelveston, directly opposite the historic Star and Garter pub. Our property holds a 9.1 Superb rating on Booking.com with 5-star guest reviews for its high quality, beautifully decorated rooms designed with fresh contemporary styling and all the necessities expected by today’s traveler.

Situated in a lovely area of Northamptonshire, Chelveston offers countryside walking, cycling, and rich points of historical interest. Guests can enjoy freshly cooked breakfasts tailored to order with the finest local ingredients.

Your Hosts: Jane & John

Dedicated to making your stay as comfortable and enjoyable as possible.

Terms & Booking Conditions

• Check-in time: Between 14:00 (2:00 PM) and 19:00 (7:00 PM) on the day of arrival. (Please inform us in advance for late arrivals).

• Check-out time: By 11:00 AM on the day of departure.

A deposit is required to confirm your booking. Deposits are refundable under the following conditions:

  • Cancellation 15 days or more prior to arrival date = Full deposit refund
  • Cancellation 14 days or less prior to arrival date = 50% deposit refund
  • Cancellation 48 hours or less prior to arrival date = No deposit refund; full booking amount due
